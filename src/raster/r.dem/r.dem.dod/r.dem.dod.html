DESCRIPTION

r.dem.dod computes a DEM of Difference (DoD), output = dem - reference, on the current computational region. Negative values are surface lowering (erosion) and positive values are surface raising (deposition). The output is the DoD that r.dem.bias, r.dem.lod, r.dem.change, r.dem.errprop, and r.dem.screen take through their dod option.

Inputs that are not aligned with the computational region, either because their resolution differs or because their cell edges are offset from the region grid, are resampled onto the region with r.resamp.interp before differencing. The interp option selects bilinear (default) or bicubic interpolation. A warning reports the resolution and origin offset of every resampled input. Inputs already on the region grid, such as the output of r.dem.nk, are differenced directly.

The output carries the r.dem DoD color palette: erosion runs yellow to orange to red to magenta as it deepens, near-zero differences are pale green, and deposition runs cyan to teal to blue to navy. The palette breaks sit at 0.1, 0.3, 0.6, and 1.0 times a saturation value R on either side of zero. range sets R. Without it, R is the 98th percentile of |DoD| rounded up to one significant digit. Differences beyond R keep the end colors.

The output metadata records the command, both source maps, the interpolation method, and R. Units are copied from dem. When both inputs have timestamps, the output receives the interval from the start of the reference timestamp to the end of the dem timestamp, so it can be registered in a space-time raster dataset with t.register.

NOTES

Differencing respects the computational region and the raster mask. Cells that are null in either input are null in the output.

When an input is finer than the computational region, interpolation samples it without aggregating the cells in between. Aggregate it first with a method like r.resamp.stats method=average if that matters for the analysis.

To compare multiple DoDs, for example before and after co-registration, pass the same range so the maps share one color scale.

EXAMPLES

Difference a co-registered post-event DEM against the reference lidar DEM:

g.region raster=elev_lid792_1m
r.dem.dod dem=dsm_post_nk reference=elev_lid792_1m output=dod

Two DoDs on a shared color scale, before and after co-registration:

r.dem.dod dem=dsm_post reference=elev_lid792_1m output=dod_raw range=1
r.dem.dod dem=dsm_post_nk reference=elev_lid792_1m output=dod_nk range=1
